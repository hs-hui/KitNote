KitNote drum model (kitnote_drums.pt)
=====================================

The model (share/ai/kitnote_drum_model.py) and its training (tools/kitnote/train_drum_model.py,
tools/kitnote/fetch_egmd_subset.py, tools/kitnote/random_grooves.py, tools/kitnote/render_kits.py,
tools/kitnote/sfz_kit.py, tools/kitnote/kit_maps.py, tools/kitnote/convert_kits.py, tools/kitnote/separated_stems.py)
are part of KitNote, GPL-3.0.

Its weights were learned from the following data. The performances (MIDI) are those of the Groove MIDI
Dataset, and grooves of many styles KitNote's random_grooves.py made; render_kits.py played them - a little
faster or slower, softer or harder - on the sampled kits below, with the microphones of each kit mixed
differently every time. Some of them separated_stems.py put into songs - a band played with MuseScore's MS
Basic SoundFont (MIT) - and took out again with Demucs (MIT), as KitNote does with a recording. The audio
changes made while training (rooms, colour, noise, tones, what a separation leaves) are KitNote's own.

Recorded performances, CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

- Groove MIDI Dataset v1.0.0 - Jon Gillick, Adam Roberts, Jesse Engel, Douglas Eck, David Bamman:
  "Learning to Groove with Inverse Sequence Transformations", ICML 2019. Google Magenta.
  https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/groove

- Expanded Groove MIDI Dataset (E-GMD) v1.0.0 - Lee Callender, Curtis Hawthorne, Jesse Engel:
  "Improving Perceptual Quality of Drum Transcription with the Expanded Groove MIDI Dataset", 2020.
  Google Magenta. https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/e-gmd  (a part of it: up to 120 minutes of each
  of its 43 kits, as fetch_egmd_subset.py chooses)
